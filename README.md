## How to read an entry

- `DR4 45` is the new Ahrefs Domain Rating, 0 to 100. Higher means the site has stronger backlinks of its own.
- `DR3 52` is the older Ahrefs Domain Rating for the same site, checked the same day.
- No chip means there is no score yet: either Ahrefs had none, or the site is still waiting for its check. It is not a zero.
- Lists are sorted by DR4, highest first. A score says nothing about how many visitors a listing sends, so track that yourself with a [campaign link](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/campaign-link-builder).
- Scores and site checks: **13 November 2026**. Check any site again for free with the [DR4 checker](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/domain-rating-checker) (up to 10 at once).

## How to pick two or three

1. **Match the audience.** Developer tool, AI app, consumer app: pick places whose front page already looks like your product.
2. **Check the wait.** Free queues range from a day to two months. Line up your launch dates in order.
3. **Check what is asked of you.** Many free routes need a badge on your site. Only agree to what you can keep.
4. **Measure visits and signups, not votes.** Give every site its own [campaign link](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/campaign-link-builder).
5. **Space them out.** One launch a week is easier to support than five on one day.

## Launch day checklist

- [ ] Signup works on phone and desktop, pricing is easy to find
- [ ] Real logo, one clear product image, one sentence that says the benefit
- [ ] Page title, description and share image are set ([check them](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/meta-tag-checker))
- [ ] AI crawlers can read your site ([check it](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/ai-crawler-checker))
- [ ] Your landing page is ready ([grade it](https://capylaunch.com/free-tools/launch-readiness-grader))
- [ ] A campaign link per site
- [ ] Time blocked to answer every comment on launch day
- [ ] A follow-up post planned with what you learned
